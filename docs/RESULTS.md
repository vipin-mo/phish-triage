## 4. Claude as a classifier

On a fixed sample of the `random` test split (1,000 emails, half phishing; Sonnet on 200 of them). Local models use their validation threshold for 1% false alarms; Claude flags an email when its phishing likelihood is 50 or more. Emails Claude didn't answer (safety refusals) are left out of its recall and false alarms here; in the cascade they fall back to DistilBERT. Cost is at standard (not batch) prices, because live triage can't wait for a batch.

## 5. The cascade: how much LLM is needed?

On 1,000 emails of the `random` test split. Latency is the single-email p50 on one CPU thread for each local stage an email passes through, plus the measured LLM call for the share that reaches it.
