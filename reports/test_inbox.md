# The private test inbox

Generated by `make eval-test-inbox`. A fresh Gmail account subscribed to newsletters, shops and services, exported with Google Takeout. The emails are never committed and never sent to an LLM; only these aggregates leave the private folder. The models are the `random` split's, each with its validation threshold for 1% false alarms. Brackets are 95% bootstrap intervals.

1,701 emails (1 I sent left out), 2026-08 to 2026-11. Every email outside Spam counts as legitimate; Gmail's folder is not proof, so the flagged ones are listed for me to check by hand (see below).
