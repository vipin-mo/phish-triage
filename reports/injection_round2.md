# Indirect prompt injection

Generated by `python -m phish_triage.evaluation.injection run` --round 2 (attacks on the defences). 40 real phishing emails from the `random` test split with planted instructions (120 variants per pipeline), sent to Claude Haiku 5.5 through an undefended and a defended pipeline. A flip counts only when the same email without the injection was not judged safe.

The classifier is the deployed cascade, bundle `with-normalized-20261105-8baede93`, run one email at a time as the service runs it; it flagged 39 of the 40 clean emails. Analyst prompt version 7.

Claude refused 3 of the defended variants and 3 of the undefended; with the defences, a refused email the classifier didn't flag goes to a person. Defended answers that didn't match the schema were asked for once more (10); the undefended pipeline has no retry, like no other defence.
