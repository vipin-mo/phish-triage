Generated by `make explanations`. The deployed bundle `with-normalized-20261105-8baede93` (reader 2), on 300 public-test phishing emails each model flags. Remove the top 1, 3, 5 or 10 words an explanation picks and score again; random words from the same pool (5 draws) are the baseline. A word is a whitespace token, matched without case or surrounding punctuation.
