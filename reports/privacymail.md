# PrivacyMail: false alarms on commercial newsletters from many senders

Generated by `make eval-privacymail`. Newsletters crowdsourced by PrivacyMail.info users (Maass, Schwär and Hollick, APF 2019; Zenodo record 6509751), used here as a test set: every email counts as legitimate. The raw data may not be shared, so only aggregates are here, and it is never sent to an LLM. The rules are in `evaluation/privacymail.py`. Brackets are 95% bootstrap intervals.

18,682 English emails from 944 websites (2019–2022), at most 25 per website after removing duplicates within each website. Read 70,882 emails to find them: 51,566 not English, 634 duplicates.

## The model deployed at the time

The bundle `with-inbox-20261104-4ee04857`, one email at a time, as the service runs it. It was replaced by the `with_newsletters` bundle, trained with part of this set; that one is measured on websites held out of its training in reports/with_inbox.md.
