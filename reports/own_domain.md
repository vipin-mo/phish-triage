# The own-domain rule: flagged, but signed by its own well-known domain

Generated by `make own-domain`. The rule (set before this ran): an email the cascade flags goes to review instead of being marked phishing when the sender check passes with a DKIM signature from the From domain itself, raises no warning, the domain is in the Tranco top 100,000, and the email has links, all on that domain. *Moved to review* counts flagged emails the rule would move; for phishing that is phishing no longer marked phishing (a person still sees it), for legitimate mail a false alarm made milder.
