# How much detection leans on the sender features

Generated by `make sender-ablation`. Stage 1 of the deployed cascade (`with-normalized-20261105-8baede93`, reader 2) refitted on the deployed split without some features, its cutoffs chosen again on validation; DistilBERT, which reads only the subject and body, is unchanged. *Without DKIM* leaves out `dkim_signed` and `dkim_aligned`, the only authentication features the models see (SPF and DMARC results are shown to me but never reach a model). *Without the header features* leaves out all 11: the sender's domain, its rank, look-alike and free-mail checks, the display name, Reply-To, To and DKIM. The private tests are scored on my laptop; only counts are shown.
