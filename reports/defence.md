# The robustness defence: reader 2 and training on attacked copies

Generated by `make defence`. Every setup runs as the service runs a bundle, one email at a time; nothing is deployed by this report. *Deployed* is the bundle in service when it ran (`with-retraining-…`, reader 1; setup *(d)* has been in service since 2026-11-05); *(a)* the same models reading with reader 2; *(b)* stage 1 retrained on `with_attacks` (the deployed split plus an attacked copy of each training phishing email) with reader 2; *(c)* DistilBERT retrained on it too. (b) and (c) change two things at once, what the models learn from (reader 2's text) and the attacked copies; *(d)*, added afterwards, retrains both stages on the deployed split's own emails read with reader 2, without copies. The private tests are scored on my laptop; only counts are shown.
